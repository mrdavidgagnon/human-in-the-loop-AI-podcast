# Episode 004 — The Feeling Learner and the Evidence Trail

Playful, dramatized interview with fictional research correspondent Dr. Rowan Field. The dialogue is a synthesis/paraphrase, not an interview with any author; no author statements or quotations are fabricated.

## Sources

1. [A taxonomy of learner-player’s emotions in serious games](https://www.zotero.org/djgagnon/items/6SLNA7ZV) — [DOI 10.17083/ijsg.v10i4.637](https://doi.org/10.17083/ijsg.v10i4.637). Evidence: conceptual taxonomy distinguishing learning-related and game-related emotions; not validation of every adaptive-emotion claim.
2. [Bridging Emotional Design and Serious Games: Towards Affective Learning Design Patterns](https://www.zotero.org/djgagnon/items/25FRUUXF) — publisher record is linked from the Zotero item. Evidence: conceptual/design framework including EmoGBL; design guidance, not a single intervention effect.
3. [The Impact of Narrative and Feedback on Cognitive Load and Learning in Educational AR Games](https://www.zotero.org/djgagnon/items/PMHSYCZM) — publisher record is linked from the Zotero item. Evidence: laboratory study with N=123; process-oriented feedback improved transfer, with bounded cognitive-load findings.
4. [PLAYFUL TESTING: designing a formative assessment game for data science](https://www.zotero.org/djgagnon/items/ME88PUW3) — book-length design account. Evidence: formative/embedded-assessment design principles; not a claim that stealth assessment is automatically fair or valid.
5. [Learning, Education, and Games, Vol. 3](https://www.zotero.org/djgagnon/items/97KF32WL) — edited-volume chapter context about mapping learning outcomes around game affordances and curriculum-supported play. Evidence: contextual design examples; chapter-level claims should be checked in the source PDF.
6. [Ontological Innovation and the Role of Theory in Design Experiments](https://www.zotero.org/djgagnon/items/CWE62TKC) — methodological context for making new constructs and theoretical moves visible in design research. It is not itself a game intervention study and is used only for the evidence-trail/methodology point.

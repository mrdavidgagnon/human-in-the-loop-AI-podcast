forms that make them legible, from an ambiguous cinematic tear to the broken
line and interrupted time of *Open Water* and the possibility of beginning
again.

## Episode synthesis

The formulations “a trace can be evidence without being a transparent
confession” and “read the form before you infer the feeling” are this episode's
synthesis of Brinkema's method, not measured psychological findings. The
episode follows Brinkema's reading of the ambiguous droplet beside Marion
Crane's eye in *Psycho* and her account of *Open Water* as an organization of
waiting, interruption, surface and depth, broken lines, intermittent sharks,
and concluding absence. It closes with Brinkema's turn to joy as formal
repetition, affirmation, and the possibility of beginning again.

Applications to learning games, trace interpretation, public media, and
research infrastructure are present-day editorial implications rather than
claims established by Brinkema. The source does not validate an inference from
a pause, click, expression, or other trace to a learner's inner state. Context,
alternative explanations, participant perspectives, evidence appropriate to
the construct, and human contestability remain necessary.

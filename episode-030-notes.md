## Episode synthesis

The episode's central distinction is between asking for an explanation and
showing that a particular explanation has been earned. A temporal beginning,
ongoing dependence, infinite causal regress, eternal past, spatial infinity,
and finite observable horizon are different claims. Physics, metaphysics, and
theology may ask related questions, but conclusions do not move between those
levels without additional premises.

Applications to AI-generated explanation, provenance, evidence boundaries, and
human review are the podcast's editorial synthesis. They are not findings in
the focal essay or evidence about a deployed AI system.

## Episode synthesis

The formulation “laughter is not innocence; ask what contradiction it reveals
and what community it builds” is this episode's synthesis, not an outcome
tested by the book. McGowan's framework motivates questions about the relation
among joke, teller, audience, target, and social order. The episode treats the
framework as a critical orientation: identify the lack/excess collision, ask
whether the laughing group is also implicated, and do not infer consequences
from laughter alone.

Applications to public media for learning, learning games, and research
infrastructure are present-day editorial implications rather than claims or
effects established by McGowan. A comic learning experience still requires
explicit learning claims and outcome evidence. A recorded laugh is not a
transparent measure of comprehension, belonging, enjoyment, or consent.

## Episode synthesis

The formulation “fantasy does not simply hide reality; it helps reality hang
together—and sometimes reveals the seam” is this episode's synthesis of
McGowan's framework, not a measured psychological finding. The episode treats
fantasy as an interpretive question: what story makes ordinary reality cohere,
what must remain impossible for that story to work, and what costs appear when
the fantasy approaches fulfillment?

Applications to public media for learning, learning games, and research
infrastructure are present-day editorial implications rather than claims or
effects established by McGowan. A revealing narrative does not by itself prove
learning, transfer, attitude change, or understanding. A coherent data story is
not transparent access to a learner's mind; alternative explanations, context,
participant perspectives, and human contestability remain necessary.

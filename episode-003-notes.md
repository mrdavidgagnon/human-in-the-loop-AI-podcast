## Sources

[Game-based structural debriefing: How can teachers design game-based curricula for systems thinking?](https://www.zotero.org/djgagnon/items/V52FZLXT) — [DOI 10.1108/ILS-05-2019-0039](https://doi.org/10.1108/ILS-05-2019-0039). 3. [Learning, Education, and Games, Vol. 3](https://www.zotero.org/djgagnon/items/97KF32WL) — edited-volume chapter context about mapping learning outcomes around game affordances and curriculum-supported play. Evidence: contextual design examples; chapter-level claims should be checked in the source PDF.

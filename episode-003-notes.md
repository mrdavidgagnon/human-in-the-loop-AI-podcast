# Episode 003 — When a Game Needs a Teacher

Playful, dramatized interview with fictional research correspondent Dr. Rowan Field. The dialogue is a synthesis/paraphrase, not an interview with any author; no author statements or quotations are fabricated.

## Sources

1. [Instructional Video and GenAI-Supported Chatbot in Digital Game-Based Learning](https://www.zotero.org/djgagnon/items/67TNQNST) — [DOI 10.1111/jcal.70094](https://doi.org/10.1111/jcal.70094). Evidence: 160 seventh-grade students, 2×2 scaffold design, science learning/cognitive load/game traces; one context, not a universal GenAI effect.
2. [Coordinating scaffolds for collaborative inquiry in a game-based learning environment](https://www.zotero.org/djgagnon/items/XDYFELXE) — [DOI 10.1002/tea.21656](https://doi.org/10.1002/tea.21656). Evidence: coordination of inquiry-cycle and accountable-talk scaffold functions; claims are bounded to the study context.
3. [The Impact of Narrative and Feedback on Cognitive Load and Learning in Educational AR Games](https://www.zotero.org/djgagnon/items/PMHSYCZM) — [DOI 10.22318/icls2025.112751](https://doi.org/10.22318/icls2025.112751) · [author institutional publication page](https://www.uni-due.de/psychologische-forschungsmethoden/flottmann_engl.php). Evidence: laboratory study with N=123; process-oriented feedback improved transfer; narrative/feedback interaction was not significant in the reported outcomes.
4. [Game-based structural debriefing: How can teachers design game-based curricula for systems thinking?](https://www.zotero.org/djgagnon/items/V52FZLXT) — [DOI 10.1108/ILS-05-2019-0039](https://doi.org/10.1108/ILS-05-2019-0039). Evidence: design-based research with teacher workshops and three teacher implementations; framework/curriculum evidence, not a universal effect estimate.
5. [PLAYFUL TESTING: designing a formative assessment game for data science](https://www.zotero.org/djgagnon/items/ME88PUW3) — book-length design account. Evidence: alignment of mechanics, learning goals, and embedded/formative assessment; design guidance rather than a single controlled outcome study.

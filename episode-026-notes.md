# Episode 026 — How Might One Live Without a Blueprint?

Playful, dramatized interview with fictional research correspondent Dr. Rowan
Field about Todd May's interpretation of Gilles Deleuze: how a philosophy can
open possibilities without supplying a life prescription, and why that refusal
leaves human judgment responsible for uncertainty and danger.

## Episode synthesis

The episode follows a bounded question: if philosophy refuses both a model of
the good life and a rule for correct action, what can it responsibly offer? In
May's reading, Deleuze creates concepts that disturb settled identities and
make neglected possibilities available to thought. Yet the world is
differentiated rather than limitless: history and conditions constrain what
can happen, and novelty does not guarantee success or ethical value.

“Possibility is not a prescription” and “Open the possibility; keep judgment
awake” are this episode's editorial formulations, not quotations from May or
empirical findings. The applications to AI systems, learning games, educational
support, research infrastructure, institutional policy, schemas, contestability,
and stop authority are present-day extensions. May does not evaluate those
systems or prescribe their design.

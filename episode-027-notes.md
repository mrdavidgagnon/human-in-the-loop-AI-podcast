## Episode synthesis

The episode follows one question through the book: can meeting an immediate
need also change who participates in understanding, deciding, and acting? It
uses Spade's framework to distinguish delivery from mutuality and to ask how
eligibility rules, helper identities, funding conditions, hidden structure,
burnout, and conflict shape power inside a project.

Applications to AI systems, dashboards, human review, contestability, and
repair are the podcast's editorial synthesis. They are not findings in the
book or evidence about a deployed AI system.

This is a bounded close reading of one handbook. The show's roughly five-source

or causal claim requiring a background source, and padding this coherent

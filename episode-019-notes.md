## Episode synthesis

The formulation “trust is not applause; it is confidence that remains open to
questions” is this episode's synthesis, not an outcome tested by the article.
The study complicates both a simple global “trust crisis” narrative and the
opposite claim that moderately high country averages make distrust irrelevant.
It distinguishes perceived competence from perceived openness and shows that
associations between political orientation and trust vary by measure and
country. Applications to public media, learning games, and national research
infrastructure are present-day editorial implications rather than article
findings.

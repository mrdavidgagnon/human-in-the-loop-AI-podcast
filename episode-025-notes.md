# Episode 025 — When Is AI Democratization More Than a Label?

Playful, dramatized interview with fictional research correspondent Dr. Rowan
Field about five tests for moving “democratizing AI” from a label toward an
operational obligation: choice, participation, responsibility, redress, and
evidence of implementation.

## Disclosure

Rowan is a fictional composite; no cited author was interviewed or is speaking
through Rowan. The dialogue is synthesis and paraphrase that preserves evidence
limits. This is an AI-generated summary that may contain errors and is not a
substitute for reading the cited chapters.

## Episode synthesis

The five-test frame—choice, participation, responsibility, redress, and
implementation evidence—is this episode's synthesis across unlike evidence
types. The chapters did not test a shared framework, agree on one definition of
democratization, or measure a common outcome. Wider technical access may matter
but does not by itself establish shared authority, meaningful refusal,
contestability, accountability, or public benefit.

Applications to public media, research infrastructure, provenance, revision
records, objection routes, and matching human authority to human responsibility
are present-day editorial implications. They are not findings about Field Day,
UW–Madison, OpenGameData, ICICLE, or any deployed system.

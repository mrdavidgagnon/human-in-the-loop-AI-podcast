# Episode 002 — Can We See Learning in the Game?

Playful, dramatized interview with fictional research correspondent Dr. Rowan Field. The dialogue is a synthesis/paraphrase, not an interview with any author; no author statements or quotations are fabricated.

## Sources

1. [SIMVA: Serious Games Learning Analytics Based on Standards and Open Code](https://www.zotero.org/djgagnon/items/LS4U9RDW) — [CEUR-WS full paper](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-3995/GBLA25_paper1.pdf). Evidence: open-source platform description and validation-oriented analytics workflow; not evidence that every generated metric measures learning.
2. [Evaluation of a framework for the integrating of Learning Analytics in Virtual Reality](https://www.zotero.org/djgagnon/items/SNR97HXI) — publisher link recorded in Zotero. Evidence: observation of seven computer-science students integrating analytics into VR; implementation/usability evidence, not a learning-gains trial.
3. [Automated detection of proactive remediation by teachers in reasoning mind classrooms](https://www.zotero.org/djgagnon/items/Z4RME73N) — [DOI 10.1145/2723576.2723607](https://doi.org/10.1145/2723576.2723607). Evidence: log-based detector of teacher remediation; inference is context-sensitive and generalization is limited.
4. [Performance Factors Analysis — A New Alternative to Knowledge Tracing](https://www.zotero.org/djgagnon/items/CP694EPH) — publisher link recorded in Zotero. Evidence: model comparison for EDM/adaptive practice; not proof of broad classroom benefit.
5. [The Difficulty of Achieving High Precision with Low Base Rates for High-Stakes Intervention](https://www.zotero.org/djgagnon/items/R4GVL9SN) — [DOI 10.1145/3706468.3706477](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706468.3706477). Evidence: methodological demonstration about prevalence, precision, and error costs; not an intervention trial.
6. [Coordinating scaffolds for collaborative inquiry in a game-based learning environment](https://www.zotero.org/djgagnon/items/XDYFELXE) — [DOI 10.1002/tea.21656](https://doi.org/10.1002/tea.21656). Evidence: high-level game-based collaborative-inquiry/scaffolding context; the episode does not claim more than the source supports.
